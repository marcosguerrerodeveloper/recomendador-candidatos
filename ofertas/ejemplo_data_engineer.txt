Data Engineer (Analytics Platform)

We are a digital bank and our analytics teams depend on data arriving on time,
correct and well documented. We are hiring a data engineer to own the batch
pipelines that feed our warehouse and to keep them trustworthy as the company
grows.

Your job will be to design, build and maintain ingestion and transformation
jobs, model the data so that analysts can answer questions without guessing,
and put quality checks in place so that a broken upstream table is caught
before anyone downstream notices it.

Our stack is Python and SQL, orchestrated with Apache Airflow. Transformations
are written in dbt on top of a cloud warehouse, and we use Apache Spark and
Kafka for the heavier and the streaming workloads. Everything runs in Docker
and lives in Git with proper code review.

We are looking for solid data modelling skills, experience with dimensional
warehouse design and the kind of engineer who documents a pipeline before
being asked to. A computer science background is welcome but we care more
about what you have actually shipped. Remote friendly within Europe.
