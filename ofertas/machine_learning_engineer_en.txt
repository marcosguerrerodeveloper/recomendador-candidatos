Machine Learning Engineer

Remote (EU time zones) · Full time
Platform team · Recommendations and personalisation

About the role

We run the recommendation and personalisation services behind a marketplace
used daily by hundreds of thousands of people. We are looking for a Machine
Learning Engineer who is as comfortable training a model as keeping it healthy
in production six months later. This is an engineering role: most of the work
is about reliability, latency and reproducibility, not about chasing the last
point of offline accuracy.

What you will do

- Own recommendation models end to end: training, evaluation, deployment and
  monitoring.
- Build reproducible training pipelines with versioned data and experiment
  tracking.
- Serve models behind low-latency APIs and keep tail latency under control.
- Detect data and concept drift, and automate retraining when it is justified.
- Run online experiments and connect offline metrics to business outcomes.
- Work closely with backend engineers on the serving infrastructure.

Requirements

- At least 3 years of experience building and deploying machine learning
  systems in production.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics or a related
  field.
- Strong Python, including the scientific stack and at least one deep learning
  framework (PyTorch or TensorFlow).
- Solid software engineering practice: version control, testing, code review,
  continuous integration.
- Experience with containers (Docker) and orchestration in a production
  environment.
- Working knowledge of SQL and of a data warehouse.
- Minimum English level C1 — this team works entirely in English.

Nice to have

- Experience with feature stores and model registries.
- Familiarity with MLflow, Kubeflow or comparable tooling.
- Exposure to recommender systems specifically (collaborative filtering,
  two-tower retrieval, learning to rank).
- Experience mentoring junior engineers.

What we offer

- Fully remote within European time zones.
- Yearly learning budget and conference attendance.
- A team that treats production incidents as engineering problems, not blame.
